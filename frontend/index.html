Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Passion

Born from a deep love of authentic Thai cooking, Thai Delight is a family-owned restaurant bringing the soul of Thailand to Springdale, Arkansas. Every recipe has been passed down through generations — crafted with the freshest local ingredients and traditional techniques that honor our heritage.

From the moment you walk in, you're welcomed by warm spices, candlelit tables, and a team that treats every guest like family. Whether it's a quiet dinner for two or a celebration with loved ones, we make every meal unforgettable.
